Surface training documents for your organization

The Training application allows you to surface Notepad documents with information about trainings relevant for your organization.

To surface Notepad documents with training material and instructions, create a tag category named Training application. Every Notepad document that is accessible by a user and tagged with a tag under the tag category Training application will be shown in the training application. You can freely name tags within the category which will be shown in-line to the link to the document.
